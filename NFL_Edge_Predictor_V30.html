V29 · Passing-leader changes: historical test

Evaluate a pregame passing-leader change proxy, not an official quarterback starter-change or injury dataset. For each team, the most recent earlier game's leading passer is compared with its established passing leader across up to four games before that. A change is marked only if both historical samples meet minimum pass-attempt requirements. This can miss actual starter changes and can mark relief appearances.

Load the NFL game schedule and QB history first. 2023–2024 selects the coefficient; 2025+ is evaluated afterward. No current-game passing data is allowed.

A detected change is not a verified starter, injury, or known pregame lineup. Retrospective leader identity may only have been observable after the preceding game; this experiment does not use announcements or real-time availability. Training and test years have already been examined by earlier app experiments, so 2025+ is not genuinely untouched across the entire project. No adjustment changes the main simulator, ATS results, or Kalshi values.
