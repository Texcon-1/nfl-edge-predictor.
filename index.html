Important limitations

This is a working simulation interface, not a calibrated NFL forecasting model. Team ratings start at 1500 and must be entered manually. The point-margin model assumes a 13.5-point standard deviation and a 2.5-point home advantage. These are prototype assumptions, not verified current-season estimates.

No live schedules, injury reports, weather, player props, Kalshi connectivity, or historical backtest yet. The market comparison is a raw percentage difference, not a guaranteed edge; fees and market rules matter. No wagering advice is implied.
